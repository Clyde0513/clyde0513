## About

Hiii, I’m Clyde! A Computer Science & Linguistics double major graduate at UCLA, Class of 2026. I build software and research systems at the intersection of full-stack engineering, machine learning, quantum computing, and natural language processing.

Currently, I am a Student Researcher working with VQEs and the QMProt dataset using PennyLane, a Tech Lead at the UCLA Quantum Computing Student Association, and a Student Researcher at Fetch.ai. Previously, I interned at UCLA Health, where I helped ship an AI-powered blood pressure digitization tool using GPT enterprise models and HandwrittenOCR. I also led development for Palisades Architects Connection through AIA.

I enjoy building practical, research-informed systems that connect strong engineering with emerging AI and computational methods. And I also enjoy playing Valorant and watching Kdramas! Some of my favorite Kdramas are My Demon, Lovely Runner, Hotel Del Luna.
